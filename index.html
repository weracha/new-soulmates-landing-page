Introducing Seoulmates

A K-drama style love story... where you and your bias are the main leads! And here's the story...

He has been your best friend since the first grade. Same school. Same class. Same apartment building now that you're adults — literally living across the hall — the boy next door.

He knocks on your door every morning like an idiot. Brings you dinner you forgot to eat. Holds you on the nights the world gets too heavy.

You've always called it friendship.
But the way he looks at you has never been just friendly.
You just never noticed.

♡

But then, Minju enters his life. Beautiful, sweet, the kind of girl who's impossible not to like. And slowly, without either of you really noticing, things between you and him begin to change. He spends more time with her. Notices you a little less. The things that used to belong to just the two of you don't feel quite so much like yours anymore. And somehow, that starts to hurt. You just don't know why yet.

That's the thing about slow-burn "friends to lovers" romance.

You don't realize you're in it until it already hurts.

✧

There's a drunk confession that spills out of you in the middle of the street at midnight — words you didn't even know you were holding.

An almost-kiss so close you can feel his breath.

A love triangle that slowly, quietly hollows you out in ways you don't understand until you're already hollowed.

A fight so bad it breaks something that feels unbreakable.

And then — the worst words you've ever said to someone you love, said to him, looking right at him.

You will want to reach through the pages and stop yourself.

You won't be able to.

✦

It keeps getting worse. The misunderstanding that could end everything. The moment you push him away so hard you're not sure he'll come back. The silence that stretches so long it starts to feel permanent.

This is a K-drama. You already know it's going to wreck you.
